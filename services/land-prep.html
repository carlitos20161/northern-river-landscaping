Land Prepping and Earth Moving

At Northern River, we excel in comprehensive land prep and earthmoving services tailored to your needs. Our expertise includes grading properties to create perfectly level landscapes, ensuring optimal drainage and a stable foundation. We specialize in preparing sites for retaining walls, carefully excavating and grading to provide solid support and stability. We also handle the delicate task of removing old ponds, carefully excavating and clearing out debris to restore or repurpose the area.

Additionally, we are experts in flattening out properties, ensuring that even the hilliest terrain is transformed into usable, aesthetically pleasing space. Our land clearing services remove unwanted brush, trees, and debris, making way for new landscaping projects or construction. We also tackle hillside stabilization, preventing erosion and maintaining the integrity of your landscape.

How We Work

Here's how we bring your land prep project from idea to reality.

STEP 1
Site Assessment

We evaluate your property to identify obstacles like trees, stumps, and uneven terrain. We discuss your vision and project goals.

STEP 2
Planning & Permitting

We develop a detailed plan, including grading, drainage, and any required permits, ensuring everything is compliant and efficient.

STEP 3
Execution

Our team clears brush, removes stumps, levels the ground, and handles any rock or debris hauling—prepping your site for the next stage.

STEP 4
Final Grading & Inspection

We perform precise grading to ensure proper drainage and a stable foundation, then walk you through a final inspection, leaving your site ready for the next phase of your project.
