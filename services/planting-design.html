How We Work

From first consultation to installation and beyond—here’s how we bring your planting and design vision to life.

STEP 1
Consultation

We begin by understanding your vision and needs. We assess your property's unique conditions—such as sunlight, soil, and climate—while also considering your preferred style.

STEP 2
Design Plan

We craft a personalized plant design tailored to your space. This includes selecting the right plants based on your location, from deer-resistant varieties to seasonal interest, ensuring a vibrant landscape.

STEP 3
Proposal & Agreement

Once the design is approved, we provide a detailed proposal, including plant selections, placement, and a timeline. After approval, we finalize the agreement and prepare for installation.

STEP 4
Installation & Care

Our team expertly installs each plant, ensuring proper spacing and healthy root development. Afterward, we guide you on care and maintenance, so your new landscape flourishes season after season.

We stand by the quality of our work, which is why we offer a one-year warranty on all plantings. Throughout the year, we provide seasonal evaluations to ensure your landscape thrives. We also prioritize sustainable practices, partnering with local nurseries and using environmentally conscious methods. With us, you're not just getting a garden; you're investing in a beautiful, lasting outdoor space. Contact us today, and let's grow something extraordinary together.
