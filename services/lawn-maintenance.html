Seasonal Landscape Care Services
SPRING
Spring Clean-Up (March – April):

As winter fades and warmer days approach, it's vital to refresh your landscape. Our spring clean-up is a comprehensive service designed to breathe new life into your yard. We meticulously remove all winter debris, including fallen leaves, twigs, and branches. Our team carefully rakes the lawn and garden beds, removing thatch and ensuring proper soil aeration. By the time we're finished, your landscape will be primed for lush spring growth and seasonal blooms.

BED
Pre-Emergent Bed Treatment (March – April):

Give your garden beds a head start with our targeted pre-emergent treatment. Applied early in the season, this vital step prevents weeds before they even begin to sprout. By creating a protective barrier in your flower and shrub beds, we safeguard the vitality of your plants, allowing them to flourish without the competition of invasive weeds. Expect cleaner, healthier, and more vibrant garden beds all season long.

SUMMER
Summer Pruning (July – September):

Keep your landscape looking its best with regular summer pruning. Our skilled enhancement crews shape and maintain ornamental trees, shrubs, and hedges. This careful pruning not only keeps your plants in perfect form but also promotes robust growth and blooming. With expert timing, your landscape will stay vibrant, balanced, and healthy all summer long.

FALL
Fall Clean-Up (October – December):

Prepare your property for winter with our thorough fall clean-up service. Our team performs a final mowing, removes fallen leaves, and clears debris from your lawn and garden beds. We also ensure proper lawn preparation so your yard is resilient through the winter, safeguarding your investment and setting the stage for a healthy spring comeback.
