Privacy Trees & Screening That Fit Bergen County Properties

Homeowners across Upper Saddle River, Wyckoff, Ramsey, and nearby towns often ask for privacy trees in Bergen County—whether they need to screen a pool, a neighbor’s windows, or a busy street. Northern River designs and installs layered evergreen screening using proven varieties suited to New Jersey winters and summer heat.

Arborvitae installation in NJ is one of our most requested solutions: fast visual coverage, a formal hedge look when spaced correctly, and straightforward long-term care when planted in the right soil and light. We also combine hemlocks, spruces, and mixed borders with deciduous accents where you want seasonal change without sacrificing winter structure.

Design, Spacing & Long-Term Health

Poor spacing and improper depth are the main reasons privacy hedges fail. We account for mature width, air flow, utilities, and drainage—so your privacy hedge installation looks full in a few seasons without overcrowding. When needed, we coordinate with land prep and grading or fencing so trees, hardscape, and drainage work together.

For a broader planting plan, see our planting & garden design services. Ready for a proposal? Request a free estimate and tell us your goals for screening and property lines.

How We Install Privacy Trees

A clear process from site visit to walkthrough.

STEP 1
Site walk & goals

We measure sightlines, note sun exposure, check setbacks, and discuss how much coverage you want year-round versus seasonally.

STEP 2
Planting plan

We recommend species, spacing, and staggered rows if needed for depth. You’ll see how the hedge will read from inside and outside the property.

STEP 3
Soil prep & install

We amend soil as needed, set proper grade for drainage, install trees at the correct depth, stake when appropriate, and establish watering guidance.

STEP 4
Care & follow-up

We review watering, pruning timing, and optional maintenance so your screening matures evenly and stays healthy in Bergen County conditions.
