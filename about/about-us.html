About Us

Northern River Landscaping — Your Trusted Landscape Partner

Our Story

Northern River Landscaping & Design is a locally owned and operated landscaping company proudly servicing Northern Bergen County, New Jersey. Founded on hard work, precision, and pride in our craft, we specialize in creating and maintaining high-end outdoor spaces that feel both timeless and functional.

What started as a small, hands-on operation has grown into a full-service landscaping and outdoor design company trusted by homeowners who expect quality, reliability, and attention to detail. We believe your property is an extension of your home, and every project—large or small—deserves the same level of care and professionalism.

Our team focuses on clean execution, clear communication, and results that speak for themselves. From weekly property maintenance and seasonal cleanups to landscape design, plantings, masonry, hardscaping, and excavation, we approach every job with a long-term mindset—building landscapes that mature beautifully over time.

At Northern River, we don't cut corners. We show up when we say we will, we treat your property with respect, and we stand behind our work. Our goal is simple: to deliver outdoor spaces that elevate your home's value, appearance, and overall experience.
