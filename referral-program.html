Referral Program

At Northern River, we're excited to reward you for spreading the word! Our referral program is simple: when you refer a friend or family member who signs a year maintenance contract or project with us, you'll receive a $170 credit toward your next project. The process is easy: simply have your referral mention your name when they contact us, or fill out the referral form on this page. Once the project is complete, you'll get your $170 reward. There's no limit—refer as many as you'd like, and thank you for being a valued part of the Northern River family.
